What the app promises, and what it does not

Periplous infers your travel history from photograph metadata. Inference is not certainty: it can miss a trip whose photographs carry no location, merge two trips into one, or place a stop imprecisely. Everything it produces can be corrected by hand, and anything it is unsure about is held back rather than shown as fact.

Periplous is not a record of where you have been in any official, legal or immigration sense, and must not be used as one. Do not rely on it for visa applications, tax residency, insurance claims or anything else where being wrong has consequences.
